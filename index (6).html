Greek in Action 🇬🇷

Rebecca's practice: learn patterns by doing, not memorising grammar terms.

Choose a category. Practise a few questions or finish them all. You'll get a clear explanation after every answer. New breakfast-lesson categories are included after the earlier practice. No grammar terminology needed.

Tip: In Greek, the article and the word ending often change together. Notice the patterns — you don't need to memorise the grammar names!
